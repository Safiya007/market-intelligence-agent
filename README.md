## 🎯 Official Udacity Rubric Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Research Framing

* **Research Objective & Scope**: The Research Brief includes a focused Research Objective that states the business question or decision the work supports, along with a clearly defined Scope of Research describing what was included and excluded.

* **Scope Definition**: The research scope covers at least two relevant dimensions such as markets/industries, competitor types, timeframe, or key constraints.

* **Research Approach**: The Research Brief documents the AI-assisted research approach, including the types of questions asked, how research effort was prioritized, and which QuickSight capabilities were used.

* **AI-Assisted Evidence**: Submission includes screenshots demonstrating AI-assisted work such as Quick Research outputs, agent conversations, queries, research results, or analysis views.

---

### 2. Analysis and Evidence

* **Key Market Insights**: The Research Brief includes **3–5 key market insights** that are relevant to the defined research objective.

* **Verifiable Evidence**: Supporting evidence is specific and verifiable, such as figures, named sources, research signals, or referenced outputs, and directly supports the associated insight.

* **Visual Evidence**: The Research Brief includes a Visual Evidence section that references relevant visualizations and explains what each visualization shows and how it supports an insight.

* **Supporting Visuals**: When visualizations are referenced, screenshots or images of the referenced visualizations are included in the submission.

---

### 3. Reliability and Executive Communication

* **Limitations and Risks**: The Research Brief documents at least **two concrete limitations or risks**, such as missing data, conflicting signals, potential bias, or assumptions.

* **Strategic Implications**: The Research Brief explains what decisions the market intelligence could inform and translates findings into appropriate strategic guidance for leadership.

* **Explicit Non-Conclusions**: The Strategic Implications section includes at least **two explicit statements about what should not be concluded** from the available evidence.
